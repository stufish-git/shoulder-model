    Medical names

    Top tendon
    Supraspinatus. Lifts the arm. Torn most often.
    Back tendon
    Infraspinatus. Turns the arm outward.
    Small back tendon
    Teres minor. Helps turn the arm outward.
    Front tendon
    Subscapularis. Turns the arm inward.
    Bony roof
    Acromion, part of the shoulder blade.
    Collarbone joint
    Acromioclavicular (AC) joint, where the collarbone meets the bony roof.
    Cushion sac
    Subacromial bursa. Lets the tendon slide under the roof.
